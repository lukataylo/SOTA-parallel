What is real. Ground: Environment Agency 1 m height survey, 2022. Building heights: the same survey's surface model. Water: the deepest level reached in our own flood simulation, run six times (three storms, with and without the fix).

What is not. The water level slider scales the water from dry up to the simulated peak so you can watch it rise. It is not a replay of the storm minute by minute. Sewers are a constant 12 mm an hour, so this shows where water collects, not a forecast. The fix was chosen for the 35 mm storm; the other two storms show how well it carries over.
